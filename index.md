# Season 3 Frostbeam Guide

## General Playstyle

Frost Beam in Season 3 is a stationary frost cannon with limited mobility.

To play the class well, a greater focus is needed on understanding the
minimum movement required to complete raid or dungeon mechanics.

Frost Beam is a burst DPS class that can specialize in either:

- **AoE damage**
- **Single-target damage**

The build can be swapped using presets depending on the encounter.

## Frost Beam Paths

### 1. Endless Mind Frost Beam

**Playstyle:** Faster uptime and more reliant on high Mastery and class
skills for damage.

Choose **Endless Mind** if you want a more forgiving Frost Beam
gameplay experience.
